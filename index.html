Custom roll quality (%) by substat
Adjust each stat to reflect typical rolls. Example: a 2% Critical Chance roll is 16.7% quality (2 ÷ 12), while a 70% Critical Damage roll is 87.5% quality (70 ÷ 80). Each input is the assumed average quality across slots of that stat.




3 · Equipment scanner & upgrade priorities


Upload multiple screenshots of equipped gear, pets or mounts. Each image represents one item detail screen. Review its two substats and exact rolled values, then compare your scanned equipment with the generated fairy build. This helps you decide what to reroll first.



Image text recognition is experimental. For each item, check the item name, category, stats and exact percentages. You can also add an item manually. Regular item Damage/Health (e.g. 442k Damage, 10.9m Health) are NOT Forge substats. Mounts use the same two Forge substat slots as gear and pets. Item base Health and Damage are not included in the priority score.







The equipment target build is separate from the PvP build selector. Build recommendations exist only for the current page session; regenerate them after reloading the page.

Model limitations
24 total slots across 12 items, with two different substats per item and at most 12 of any single substat. Roll values are configurable (100%, 80%, or per-substat custom quality); exact per-item roll variation is approximated. Fairy effects, Double Chance timing and the 4-second Range vs Melee approach are modeled assumptions, not verified game rules. Inconclusive 10-second fights count as DRAW. Never farm for hours based on an unverified model.
