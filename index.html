Model limitations
24 total slots across 12 items, with two different substats per item and at most 12 of any single substat. Roll values are assumed to be maximal. Fairy effects and Double Chance timing are modeled assumptions, not verified game rules. Inconclusive 10-second fights count as DRAW. Never farm for hours based on an unverified model.
